Learning Facilitator & Student Leader

Rudranil Mondal

B.Sc. in Agricultural Economics | MS Candidate in Rural Development at GAU

My background combines academic economics research, campus tech leadership, and youth program management. During my studies, I completed a research internship in the Agricultural Economics Section at the Bangladesh Rice Research Institute (BRRI), gaining practical experience in field survey design, farm economics data collection, and report writing.

Alongside my studies, I served as the founding Convenor, General Secretary, and Advisor of the IT Society at Gazipur Agricultural University (GAU), where I led and coordinated over 14 events engaging more than 7,500 students. I have also served as Campus Director for the Hult Prize at GAU (recognized among the Top 25% OnCampus Programs in the region) and as On-Campus Coordinator for the Hult Prize Bangladesh national team, supporting onboarding across 73+ campuses nationwide. Additionally, I am a co-author of EasyBio Botany and EasyBio Zoology, designed to assist students preparing for university admissions.

I am a young, continuously learning facilitator with practical experience and useful knowledge, and I would be happy to contribute through training, workshops, and peer-learning sessions.

Facilitation Philosophy

My facilitation approach is grounded in peer-to-peer learning, practical trial-and-error, and hands-on skill-building. Rather than lecturing as an expert, I approach every session as a collaborative space where participants learn step-by-step through structured guides, live demonstrations, and practical exercises. My goal is to make digital tools, legal requirements, and essential career skills approachable, clear, and actionable for students, campus leaders, and early-stage entrepreneurs.
